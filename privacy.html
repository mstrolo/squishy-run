Squishy Run: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 (deleting your account from inside the game added)

Squishy Run is a game. We collect as little as possible, and nothing that identifies you personally.

What stays on your device

Your progress (best distance, coins, skins, upgrades, missions, trophies and settings) is saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The worldwide leaderboard

If the online leaderboard is turned on, the game sends the nickname you type in, your distance, how long the run lasted and a random player number (not linked to your phone, account or identity) to our leaderboard server, so everyone can see the top runs. Please don't use your real full name as your nickname. To remove your entry, contact us at the address below.

Your player account

To race online, add friends and play in the league, the game makes an anonymous player account for your phone. There is no email, password or phone number: just a random account number, the username you pick and the look of your ball. Other players can see your username. Please don't use your real full name as your username.

Signing in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, your player account is linked to your Google account so you can get your game back on a new phone. We receive your Google account's email address and name from Google, and nothing else. Your game progress (level, coins, items, stars and settings) is then also kept on our server, so it can be restored. You never have to sign in to play.

Friends

When you add a friend by their username, we store that link so you can see which friends are online and invite them to a race. While you are in the game, your friends can see that you are online.

Races and the weekly league

During a race (up to 4 players), the game sends the other racers your username, the look of your ball and where you are on the track. After a race we store your finishing place and points for the weekly league table, which every player can see. A recording of your race (username, ball look and positions, no personal information) may be saved so other players can race it later.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account yourself in the game: tap your name on the main menu, then DELETE MY ACCOUNT. Your username, friends, race results, leaderboard runs, cloud save and sign-in are deleted at once. If you no longer have the game, see how to ask us to delete your account.

What we don't do

No ads, no in-app purchases, no location, no contacts, no camera or microphone, no tracking and no selling of any data. The game doesn't ask for any permissions to your personal data.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. Online, it only stores a username, scores and race results. There is no chat.
