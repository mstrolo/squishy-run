JETRIFT - Privacy Policy

Developer: Strolo Studios. Last updated: 21 November 2026.

JETRIFT is a multiplayer shooter for Android and PC. This page says what the game keeps about you, why, and how to delete it. The game has no advertising and no analytics or tracking kits, and it does not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

What the game keeps

WhatWhyWhere
An account id: a random-looking code made from your device's identifier by a one-way hash. The device identifier itself never leaves your device.So your name, rank and progress are still there the next time you play.Your device and our database.
The player name you choose, and your clan tag if you join a clan.Shown to other players in matches, on scoreboards and in friend and clan lists.Your device and our database.
Game record: experience, kills, deaths, assists, captures, games and wins; season pass progress; the character look you chose.Rank, the season pass and clan statistics.Your device and our database.
Friends, friend requests, clan membership and clan invitations.The friends and clans features.Our database.
Presence: that you are online, and which game room you are in.So friends can see you and join you. Removed ten minutes after you stop playing.Our database.
Your IP address.Needed to connect you to a game room. It is not stored with your account. If you host a room yourself, your IP address and port are listed so that others can join it, and removed within ten minutes of the room closing.Game servers while connected; the room list while you host.
The dates your account was created and last used.To find the right account when a code is restored on another phone.Our database.
Voice chat, when you turn the microphone on.Sent live to the players in your room. It is not recorded or stored.Passes through the game server.
Reports you send about another player: your account id, the name that player wore, the reason and the room.So we can act on abuse.Our database.

The game does not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or payment details.

Permissions

Microphone: only for voice chat, only while you hold or switch on TALK. Voice chat can be turned off in SETTINGS, and you can mute or block any player from the scoreboard.
Internet: to play online.

Who processes the data

The database is hosted by Supabase. Game rooms run on servers rented by the developer. They process the data above only to run the game.

Other players

Your player name, clan tag, scores and voice (when your microphone is on) are seen or heard by the players you play with. In a match you can open the scoreboard and tap a player to mute, block or report them.

Children

JETRIFT is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your account and data

In the game: open SETTINGS on the main menu and tap DELETE ACCOUNT twice. This deletes your name, record, friends, clan membership and season pass progress from our database at once.
Without the game: use the account deletion page with the account code shown on the game's main menu.
Reports you sent about other players are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.
