Delete your JETRIFT account

Developer: Strolo Studios. Deleting an account removes, at once and for good: the player name, the game record (experience, kills, deaths, assists, captures, games, wins), friends and friend requests, clan membership and invitations, season pass progress and presence. If you led a clan, it passes to its oldest member, or is disbanded when you were the last one. Reports you sent about other players are kept for up to 12 months.

In the game

Open SETTINGS on the main menu and tap DELETE ACCOUNT twice.

On this page

Type the account code shown on the game's main menu (CODE, eight letters and digits, like 3F9A-2B7C).
